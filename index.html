Pyramid Solitaire Rules

Objective: Clear all cards from the pyramid.

  • Remove pairs of available cards that total 13.
  • Card values: Ace=1, 2-10 face value, J=11, Q=12, K=13.
  • Kings (value 13) can be removed by themselves.
  • Available cards are those not covered by any other card in the pyramid, or the top card of the Waste pile.
  • Click the Stock pile (top left) to deal a card to the Waste pile (next to it).
  • You can reset the Waste pile back to the Stock once per game when the Stock is empty.
